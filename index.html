Dynamics Starter Pack

A hands-on warm-up for Steven Strogatz’s Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos. You will drag, predict, and press your way through iteration, chaos, and bifurcations, then cross over to flows, phase planes, and the Lorenz attractor, so that when you open the book, chapters 1–10 already feel familiar.

Touch first, name second, equation third. All you need is one-variable calculus.
